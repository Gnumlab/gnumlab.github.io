# Hello there, I'm Luca Pepè Sciarria

I am a post-doc at University of Rome Tor Vergata. My research focuses on Algorithm Engineering with a focus on Data Mining tasks alongside a strong interest in Theoretical Computer Science aspects.

In my post-doc period I was and am lucky to work with Prof. [Andrea Clementi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=yoiAbLMAAAAJ&hl=it) who introduced me to the fascinating world of randomness and Prof. [Luciano Gualà](https://lucianoguala.github.io/homepage/) who constantly shares his entusiasm for data structure with me.\
I received my PhD from University of Rome Tor Vergata, where I was fortunate to be advised by [Giuseppe F. Italiano](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=6jiwt-UAAAAJ&hl=fr) and really unfortunate to be a PhD student during COVID-19.

My interests in computer science do not stop there. I am appasionate about the actual practical impact of the theory and how it is reflected in real systems, where the Big-O notation does not tell the whole story.\
I am a strong advocate for the `C` programming language and for low-level aspect of computer science. As a result, my research life ranges from Chernoff bounds to segmentation faults and back.

I am always hungry! Some time for knowledge, but most of the time for food! My personal interests includes eating and cooking. I am eager to cook new plates of food and try to experiment with spices and recipes.
Cooking is like research: it makes sense if you share it with others.
In the future I will add some of my creations here.

I am also a bookworm. I love reading. My favourite genres are historical fiction and fantasy. I am a big fun of **Patrick O'Brian** novels and **Malazan** epopea, and I'm also a **Terry Pratchett** fan!\
I'm currently reading **Stonewielder** by Ian Esslemont.
